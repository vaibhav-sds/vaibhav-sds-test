Vaibhav Agarwal

Social data scientist · Doctoral researcher

Technical University of Munich

I'm a doctoral researcher at the Professorship for Algorithmic Governance and Public Policy at the Munich School of Politics and Public Policy, Technical University of Munich. My interests bring together social data science, human behaviour, and public governance.

I study how people perceive and respond to algorithmic systems in public services, particularly how they form trust, use information, and make decisions. I use conjoint experiments and behavioural data to look beyond the final survey response: how people arrive at a choice can tell us something that the choice alone does not.

I also work with text, images, audio, and video, and develop tools for collecting and analysing social data. My projects include processing short-video feeds, extracting account names with OCR, and working with political text. I primarily work in Python and R, and enjoy making research accessible through clear visualisations, presentations, and teaching.

I grew up in Bangalore, where I studied Economics at Azim Premji University, with a minor in Data, Democracy, and Development. I then moved to Finland for a master's in Contemporary Societies, specialising in Social Data Science at the University of Helsinki. While there, I worked as a research assistant on AGAPP and HEPP projects.

At TUM, I teach Social Data Science and Public Opinion Research Methods. I especially enjoy helping students from different disciplinary backgrounds work with data and computational methods.
